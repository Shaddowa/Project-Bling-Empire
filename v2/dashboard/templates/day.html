How to read it

Shortlist = your holdings + fresh swing setups + long-term BUY/WATCH. 15-minute bars, fetched live when you open this page. ORB = first-30-min opening range: price above it with volume is intraday strength, below is weakness. VWAP = the day's fair price — longs above, not below. This page is decision support for timing an entry/exit you already planned; it will never say "day-trade this". Day trading against professionals is where retail bleeds — use it to place planned trades better, not to find new ones.
