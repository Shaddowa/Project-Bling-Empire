How the pushes reach your phone

Notifications go through ntfy.sh, a free push relay in the cloud — your phone never talks to the VPS. The VPS publishes to a secret channel ("topic"); your phone subscribes to the same topic in the ntfy app. Anyone who knows the topic name can read it, so it's a long random string — treat it like a password.

Setup — two minutes, once

  1. Install ntfy from the App Store (white bell on green).
  2. Open it → + → paste the topic below (server stays ntfy.sh).
  3. Allow notifications when iOS asks.
  4. Press the test button here — your phone should buzz within seconds.
